HeyTora

An AI companion for Windows. Hold a key and ask - Tora hears you, looks at your screen when it needs to, and points at what to click to show you how.

Download for Windows
Version 1.7.0 · 193 MB · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit A test build. All versions

Getting started

  1. Run Setup-HeyTora.exe. It installs just for you - no admin password. Windows may say it doesn't recognise the app, because this test build isn't signed yet: click More info, then Run anyway. Your browser may also ask you to Keep the download.
  2. Sign in. HeyTora works with your HeyTora account: on the first launch, type its email - we email you a link, click it and you're in, on your plan. Once signed in you can also think with your own Google Gemini key (aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) instead.
  3. Hold Ctrl + Win and talk. Allow the microphone the first time.

HeyTora sends what you say - and, when it needs to look, a picture of your screen - to Google's Gemini - through HeyTora's server when you're signed in, or straight to Google with your own key - and web searches to a search engine. Uninstall any time from Settings → Apps.
